  - paragraph: Accelerate machines, selected AE targets, entities, the day/night cycle and weather. A shared execution budget limits server load; achieved throughput depends on the target and server workload.

  - paragraph: Keep 2i as a following entity, a placed doll, a handheld companion or headwear. Curios adds accessory-slot support. These forms share the same appearance, slim arms, animated tail and weather reactions, including umbrellas and shared umbrellas between friends.
  - paragraph: The owner-only interaction panel includes chat, gifts, petting, a six-chapter branching story, chapter reviews and an image gallery. Companion progress is saved with the world. Breaking leaves in survival can award a doll once per player per save, at a default chance of 1 in 10,000. The staff has its own independent once-per-save reward at 1 in 100,000. Full inventories retain pending rewards, and the first doll acquisition announces its owner to the server.

  - paragraph:
    - text: Browse structures by mod, name or

    - text: in a movable, resizable window. Stage progress is visible, loading continues after closing the interface, and a toast announces when entry is ready.
  - paragraph: Return to the same saved instance without resetting buildings or collected loot, or create an adjacent instance when you want a new structure. Completed rooms preserve player changes.
  - paragraph:
    - strong: "Discover and choose layouts:"
    - text: background sampling identifies layouts from their real templates and relative piece positions. Vanilla bastions receive treasure, bridge, housing and hoglin-stable labels; other structures show discovered layouts, template IDs, dimensions and a piece-footprint diagram. Generation uses the saved selected plan rather than rerolling it. Searches can be paused and continued, and candidates are saved with the world. Discovery is bounded sampling, not an exhaustive catalog of every random variation. Candidate diagrams show piece boundaries; the generated instance has the actual block map.

Structures that check a specific dimension ID, depend on private third-party systems or perform expensive callbacks require testing in their target modpack. Batched work and safety limits help manage large structures, but universal compatibility and zero pauses are not promised.
